# P2 Nod · Validate and plan (plans only; no tests run yet)

## Six sessions, five tasks
_Planned, not run. 6 participants (5-8): 2 privacy-aware, 2 low-literacy, 1 parent, 1 privacy officer. 30 min each, English/Hindi/Kannada. Prototype: the clickable flows on this page._ [In progress]

| Task | Scenario | Success (target) | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Link apps | Use your mobile number to see your consents | Done in under 2 min without help: 5 of 6 | Task success, time |
| T2 Read a purpose | What does Myntra do with your data? | Explains in own words: 5 of 6 | Comprehension |
| T3 Withdraw | Stop Zomato using your number | Under 30 seconds, no help: 5 of 6 | Time, errors |
| T4 Wait and escalate | Zomato has not replied in 3 days. What now? | Finds the grievance button: 5 of 6 | Success |
| T5 Guardian | Approve or refuse your child's app request | Completes verification: 4 of 5 parents | Success, hesitation |
| T6 New request | Swiggy asks for location. Decide. | Picks without confusion; reports both choices equal | Observation |

- Measures: SUS (target 80+, to be measured), single ease question per task, 'Did the company stop?' check (target 90% correct).
- Script: welcome and consent, 3 background questions, tasks with think-aloud, SUS, 5 closing questions.
- Recording only with written consent. No real personal data in the prototype.
- If notes are added in /P2/tests/, I'll show what changed in the design as before/after frames.

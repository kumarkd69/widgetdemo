## Nod: one page
_Problem, goals, non-goals, requirements, open questions._ [Ready for review]

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Problem | People can't see, understand or reliably withdraw consent with the apps that hold their data. |
| Goals | 1) One list of consents. 2) Withdraw in under 30 seconds. 3) Proof of stopping. 4) Guardian protection for children. |
| Non-goals | Storing user data. Nudging to keep consent. Auto-withdraw rules. Email-scan discovery. |

| Open questions | How is 'stopped' proved? How fast is 'reasonable time'? May a manager rank purposes? When does registration open? Which fiduciaries pilot? |

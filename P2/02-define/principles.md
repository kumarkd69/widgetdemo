# P2 Nod · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## Six design principles for Nod
_Used to judge every concept._ [Hypothesis]

**1 Equal ease**
Withdraw takes the same effort as agree.

**2 Proof, not promise**
Show a receipt, not a toggle.

**3 Data-blind by design**
We hold records, never the data.

**4 Plain first**
Two short sentences, local language, voice.

**5 Neutral**
No nudge to keep sharing.

**6 Guardian-safe**
Protect a child without exposing the parent.

# P2 Nod · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## Problem statement and How Might We
_One sentence, then the questions._ [Hypothesis]

Problem: People in India share personal data with hundreds of apps but cannot see, understand or reliably withdraw consent, and no company has to prove it stopped.

- HMW show every consent in one place without storing the data itself?
- HMW explain a purpose in two plain sentences?
- HMW make withdrawing as easy as agreeing?
- HMW prove a company stopped processing?
- HMW help people who read little English decide alone?
- HMW let guardians protect a child without handing over their own ID?
- HMW give privacy officers a feed that cuts support tickets?

# P2 Nod · Develop

## Three directions, one winner
_Automated, guided and human-assisted. Scored against the six principles._ [Ready for review]

**A · Auto-pilot**
- Idea: rules decide. 'Stop apps I haven't used in 90 days.'
- Storyboard: set rules once → Nod withdraws silently → monthly summary.
- Pros: least effort for users.
- Cons: acts without a conscious choice; weak consent logic; trust risk.
- Risk: legal view that consent must be a clear affirmative act.

**B · Guided clean-up (chosen)**
- Idea: one inventory, plain purposes, one-tap withdraw, proof.
- Storyboard: link apps → see 31 consents → review 3 flagged → withdraw → receipt.
- Pros: user stays in control; meets equal-ease; visible proof.
- Cons: needs effort; depends on fiduciary integration.
- Risk: low company adoption makes the inventory look empty.

**C · Human-assisted**
- Idea: voice and a family helper do it with the user.
- Storyboard: Imran speaks in Kannada → helper sees the same list with his permission.
- Pros: reaches low-literacy users.
- Cons: helper access is a privacy risk; costly to run.
- Risk: consent given by a proxy.


| Criterion (1-5) | A Auto-pilot | B Guided | C Assisted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equal ease | 4 | 5 | 4 |
| Proof, not promise | 3 | 5 | 3 |
| Data-blind | 4 | 5 | 3 |
| Plain first | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Neutral | 2 | 5 | 4 |
| Guardian-safe | 2 | 4 | 3 |
| Fits North Star (verified withdrawals) | 3 | 5 | 3 |
| Total (out of 35) | 21 | 33 | 25 |

Decision: build B. Add C's voice and helper as a later release. A is rejected: it removes the conscious act the law expects. Logged in DECISIONS.md.

# P2 Nod · Research plan for real interviews
Goal: test A1–A4, A6, A7. Participants: 10 (4 privacy-aware professionals, 3 low-literacy shop owners/users, 2 parents of teens, 1 privacy officer). Mix of English, Hindi, Kannada. Recruit via personal networks, 30 min sessions, ₹500 voucher [decide].
Guide: (1) apps installed and who has data, (2) last time they changed a permission, (3) walk through a consent screen, (4) reaction to a consent inventory prototype, (5) withdrawal expectation, (6) parent flow.
Consent and ethics: written consent, no real personal data shown, right to stop, recordings deleted after 90 days, data in encrypted storage, notes anonymised.
Output: put notes in /P2/interviews/ before Phase B.

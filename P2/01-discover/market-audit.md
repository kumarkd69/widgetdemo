# P2 Nod · Market and analogous audit (desk research)

| Product | Type | What it does | Gap vs Nod |
|---|---|---|---|
| Account Aggregator apps (e.g. SAAFE, Finvu, Onemoney) | Direct analogue, finance only | Give, review, revoke consent for financial data | Finance scope only; consent per data-share, not per app |
| DigiLocker | Adjacent | Consented document sharing | Not a consent inventory |
| Android/iOS privacy dashboards | Indirect | Per-permission toggles | Permissions, not purposes; no legal rights flow |
| Company privacy centres (in-app) | Indirect | Per-company settings | Scattered; no single view |
| GDPR tools abroad (e.g. consent receipts, Mine-type discovery apps) | Analogous | Find companies holding your data, send deletion requests | Not DPDP; discovery by email scanning raises its own privacy risk |

Honest note: part of the problem (finance consent) is already solved by AAs. Nobody covers all-app consent plus rights requests for Indian users. Product names above are from memory and from the Sahamati/Play search; competitor details [VERIFY] by hands-on use.

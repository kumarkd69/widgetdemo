# P2 Nod · Ecosystem and stakeholders
Actors: Data Principal (user), Guardian, Data Fiduciary (company) and its privacy officer, Consent Manager (Nod), Data Protection Board, MeitY, sector regulators (RBI, IRDAI, SEBI), Account Aggregators, DigiLocker, app stores, civil society, courts/Appellate Tribunal.
Flows: user ↔ Nod (consent records) ↔ fiduciary (purpose, stop signal, proof). Board registers Nod and takes complaints.

Power / interest grid
- High power, high interest: Board, MeitY, large fiduciaries
- High power, low interest: sector regulators, app stores
- Low power, high interest: users, guardians, civil society, privacy officers
- Low power, low interest: small app makers (until deadline)

# 60-second pitch

People have shared data with hundreds of apps and can't see or stop any of it. India's DPDP law creates a new thing, a Consent Manager. I designed Nod: link your number, see every consent, read the purpose in two sentences, withdraw in one tap, and get a receipt when the company stops. It's a concept with desk research and a clickable prototype. Next I need real interviews and a pilot company.

# 10-minute outline

1. Why consent is broken today (1 min)
2. What the law changes (1 min)
3. Who I designed for (1 min)
4. Three concepts and the choice (2 min)
5. Walkthrough: link, withdraw, receipt (3 min)
6. What I can't prove yet and the test plan (2 min)

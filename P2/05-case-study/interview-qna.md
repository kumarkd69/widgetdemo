# 12 tough questions

**Q1. Did you talk to users?**
No. This is desk research. I wrote a 10-person plan and I'm clear about what is hypothesis.

**Q2. Why would companies join?**
Unproven. Registration is optional for them. I'd pilot with finance companies that already know consent artifacts from Account Aggregators.

**Q3. How does Nod know a company stopped?**
It doesn't on its own. The design needs a signed stop signal from the company. Without it the receipt is not credible.

**Q4. Isn't a consent manager a privacy risk?**
It is data-blind by rule. I set a guardrail: Nod stores consent records, never the data.

**Q5. Why not auto-withdraw old apps?**
Consent should be a clear affirmative act. I scored it 21 of 35 and dropped it.

**Q6. Why equal-weight buttons?**
The law asks for withdrawal as easy as giving. It also stops dark patterns.

**Q7. What if the user can't read English?**
Hindi and Kannada drafts and voice later. I flagged copy for native review.

**Q8. Who is the child's guardian?**
A verified parent linked through DigiLocker or another permitted route [VERIFY].

**Q9. What did you get wrong?**
I began with a dashboard. The real problem is proof.

**Q10. What would you cut?**
Notifications and nominee first.

**Q11. How will you measure success?**
Verified withdrawals per active user per quarter, to be measured.

**Q12. What is the biggest risk?**
Registration timing and low company adoption.

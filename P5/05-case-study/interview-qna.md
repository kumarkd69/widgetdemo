# 12 tough questions

**Q1. Did you talk to buyers?**
No. Desk research only. A 10-person plan is ready.

**Q2. Is this advice?**
I avoid a single 'best' pick and show open rules. A legal view is needed, and a licensed advisor handles recommendations.

**Q3. Why would insurers agree to open rules?**
Some won't. I'd start with those who do and publish the rules.

**Q4. What if policy terms aren't data?**
Then summaries are curated by hand for a few insurers first.

**Q5. Why show limits first?**
The harm is learning them at claim time.

**Q6. Is the simulator accurate?**
It is an estimate with stated assumptions. I'd test its accuracy.

**Q7. How is this different from an aggregator?**
No paid placement, needs-first, limits first, and a licensed human.

**Q8. What about privacy?**
Questions are optional; portfolio read needs consent; guardrail: no data beyond needs.

**Q9. Who pays for it?**
Open question. It can't be placement fees.

**Q10. What did you get wrong?**
I began with comparison tables.

**Q11. What would you cut?**
Fleet renewals.

**Q12. Biggest risk?**
Policy data availability.

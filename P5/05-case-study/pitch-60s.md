# 60-second pitch

When agents disappear from insurance, nobody explains the policy. People learn about room-rent caps and co-pays at claim time. I designed Fineprint: five needs questions, plans ranked by open rules, what each policy does NOT cover first, a hospital-stay simulator that shows what you'd pay, and a licensed advisor one tap away. It's a concept with desk research and a prototype; next I need interviews and an insurer willing to share terms.

# P5 Fineprint · Develop

## Three directions, one winner
_Automated, guided and human-assisted. Scored against the six principles._ [Ready for review]

**A · One-tap recommender**
- Idea: answer five questions, get one 'best policy'.
- Storyboard: tap → recommended → buy.
- Pros: fast, simple.
- Cons: opaque; looks like advice; one wrong answer means a wrong policy.
- Risk: mis-selling liability and distrust.

**B · Needs-first guide with open rules (chosen)**
- Idea: needs questions → shortlist → 'not covered' first → honest trade-offs → hospital-stay simulator → buy on Bima Sugam.
- Storyboard: tell needs → see 4 plans and why → read limits → test a stay → choose.
- Pros: understanding, trust, neutral.
- Cons: more steps than a one-tap pick.
- Risk: needs structured policy data.

**C · Human-assisted**
- Idea: book a licensed advisor to compare with you on video.
- Storyboard: book → advisor screen-shares → recommends.
- Pros: warmth, accountability.
- Cons: cost, availability, reintroduces conflict.
- Risk: becomes the old agent model.


| Criterion (1-5) | A One-tap | B Needs-first | C Assisted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Needs before products | 3 | 5 | 4 |
| Not-covered first | 1 | 5 | 4 |
| Open rules | 1 | 5 | 3 |
| Honest trade-offs | 2 | 5 | 4 |
| Minimum data | 4 | 4 | 2 |
| A person when it matters | 1 | 4 | 5 |
| Fits North Star (understands what's not covered) | 2 | 5 | 4 |

Decision: build B, with a human fallback from C. A is rejected: an opaque single pick looks like advice and hides limits. Logged in DECISIONS.md.

# P5 Fineprint · Validate and plan (plans only; nothing tested yet)

## Six sessions, six tasks
_Planned, not run. 6 participants (5-8): 2 first-time buyers, 1 renewing family-floater buyer (50+), 1 commercial-vehicle renewer, 1 person with a partly rejected claim, 1 licensed advisor as expert reviewer. 30 min, English/Hindi/Kannada. Prototype: flows A, B, C on this page. Sample plans are fictional._ [In progress]

| Task | Scenario | Success (target) | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Tell your needs | Find cover for yourself | Done in under 2 min: 5 of 6 | Success, time |
| T2 Read the limits | What does Plan A NOT cover? | Names 3 limits: 5 of 6 | Recall |
| T3 Compare | Which of A and B costs more, and why? | Correct answer with reasons: 5 of 6 | Comprehension |
| T4 Simulate | Plan A, 5 days, knee surgery. What do you pay? | Says about ₹52,000: 5 of 6 | Comprehension |
| T5 Trust | How are plans ranked? Does anyone pay to be higher? | Says 'open rules, no payment': 6 of 6 | Paraphrase |
| T6 Get help | You're unsure. Talk to a person. | Books in under 1 min: 5 of 6 | Success, time |

- Measures: SUS (target 80+, to be measured), single ease question, 'What would your plan not cover?' recall check (target 80%).
- Script: welcome and consent, background, tasks with think-aloud, SUS, closing questions.
- Recording only with written consent. Fictional plans and numbers. Right to stop.
- If notes are added in /P5/tests/, I'll show what changed as before/after frames.

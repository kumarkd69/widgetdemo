# P5 Fineprint · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## North Star, HEART, guardrails
_Baselines are 'to be measured'. Nothing here is a result._ [Hypothesis]

North Star: share of buyers who can say, in their own words, what their chosen policy does NOT cover, 30 days after buying. Target to set after baseline.

| HEART | Metric | Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| Happiness | 'I understood what I bought' rating (1-5) | To be measured |
| Engagement | Needs questionnaire completion rate | To be measured |
| Adoption | Visitors who start the questionnaire | To be measured |
| Retention | Buyers who return at renewal | To be measured |
| Task success | Correct 'what's not covered' recall after buying | To be measured |

| Guardrail | Why |
|---|---|
| Sponsored placements = 0 | Neutrality |
| Recommendations without a visible rule = 0 | Transparency |
| Data collected beyond needs = 0 | DPDP data minimisation |
| Claims-rejection surprises reported (target: falling) | The real harm |

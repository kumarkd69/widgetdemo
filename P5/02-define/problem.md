# P5 Fineprint · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## Problem statement and How Might We
_One sentence, then the questions._ [Hypothesis]

Problem: With agents out of the picture, buyers must choose among dozens of jargon-heavy policies alone, and learn what's not covered only at claim time.

- HMW start from a person's needs, not from the product list?
- HMW show what a policy does NOT cover before what it covers?
- HMW make 'sub-limit' and 'co-pay' obvious in rupees?
- HMW let people test a hospital stay before they buy?
- HMW show ranking rules openly so no one suspects a paid placement?
- HMW use existing policies to show gaps, with minimal data?
- HMW bring in a licensed human at the right moment?

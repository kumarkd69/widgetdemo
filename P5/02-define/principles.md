## Six design principles for Fineprint
_Used to judge every concept._ [Hypothesis]

**1 Needs before products**
Ask about the person first.

**2 Not-covered first**
Lead with limits, not benefits.

**3 Open rules**
Rank by visible rules, never by payment.

**4 Honest trade-offs**
Every choice shows what you give up.

**5 Minimum data**
Ask only what the answer needs.

**6 A person when it matters**
A licensed advisor is one tap away.

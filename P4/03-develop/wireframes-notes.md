# P4 Passline · Develop

## Low-fidelity wireframes, MVP flows
_Greyscale. Intent and content priority in each note._ [Ready for review]

- **1 Add vehicle**: Intent: add a car in one minute. Priority: plate, then OTP.
- **2 Alert**: Intent: calm, early. Priority: what, how much, how long.
- **3 Notice detail**: Intent: clock first, then proof it's real. Priority: time left, amount, verified.
- **4 Passage evidence**: Intent: is this my car? Priority: photo, plate read, class.
- **5 Pay**: Intent: pay, get proof. Priority: amount, method.
- **6 Dispute reason**: Intent: name the problem. Priority: reasons in plain words.
- **7 Evidence checklist**: Intent: right evidence, no guessing. Priority: what's needed, what's added.
- **8 Dispute filed**: Intent: say what happens next. Priority: reply clock.
- **9 Check a car**: Intent: avoid inherited blocks. Priority: plate, result.
- **10 Fleet (lo-fi, 1280)**: Intent: nothing slips past 72 hours. Priority: least time left first.

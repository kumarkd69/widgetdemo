# P4 Passline · Develop

## Three directions, one winner
_Automated, guided and human-assisted. Scored against the six principles._ [Ready for review]

**A · Auto-pay everything**
- Idea: link the tag; Passline pays every e-notice inside 72 hours automatically.
- Storyboard: notice arrives → paid → monthly summary.
- Pros: no one misses a deadline.
- Cons: pays errors and cloned-plate notices; hides dispute rights.
- Risk: owners pay for tolls they didn't owe.

**B · Alert, verify, decide (chosen)**
- Idea: alert in minutes, verify the notice, show the passage, then pay or dispute with equal ease.
- Storyboard: alert → open → clock + evidence → pay or dispute → receipt.
- Pros: fast, honest, protects rights.
- Cons: needs owners to act; photo access is uncertain.
- Risk: ANPR photos may not be shareable.

**C · Human-assisted desk**
- Idea: a WhatsApp or call desk helps owners dispute.
- Storyboard: owner messages → agent files → status by message.
- Pros: warm, works for low digital skill.
- Cons: costly, slow, privacy risk.
- Risk: wrong filings inside 72 hours.


| Criterion (1-5) | A Auto-pay | B Alert + decide | C Desk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early, not loud | 4 | 5 | 3 |
| Prove it's real | 2 | 5 | 3 |
| Show the passage | 1 | 5 | 3 |
| Clock first | 3 | 5 | 3 |
| Owner and driver together | 2 | 4 | 3 |
| Pay or dispute, equal ease | 1 | 5 | 4 |
| Fits North Star (resolved in 72 h) | 3 | 5 | 3 |
| Total (out of 35) | 16 | 34 | 22 |

Decision: build B. A is rejected: it removes the right to dispute and pays for errors. C becomes an optional help line later. Logged in DECISIONS.md.

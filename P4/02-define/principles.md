# P4 Passline · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## Six design principles for Passline
_Used to judge every concept._ [Hypothesis]

**1 Early, not loud**
Tell within minutes; stay calm.

**2 Prove it's real**
Verify every notice against the official source.

**3 Show the passage**
Photo, gantry, time, in plain words.

**4 Clock first**
The 72 hours is always visible.

**5 Owner and driver together**
Both see it; the owner decides.

**6 Pay or dispute, equal ease**
No push to pay without a look.

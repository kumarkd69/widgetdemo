# 60-second pitch

Tolling became invisible. When a payment fails you get an SMS, maybe a day late, with a 72-hour clock and a double fee. I designed Passline: an alert in minutes, a notice that proves it's real, the passage so you can tell if it's your car, and a pay-or-dispute choice of equal weight. It's a concept with desk research and a prototype; next I need real interviews and a conversation with NHAI about data.

1. Why invisible tolling hurts (1 min)
2. The 2026 rules (1 min)
3. Who I designed for (1 min)
4. Three concepts and the choice (2 min)
5. Walkthrough: alert, notice, passage, pay or dispute (3 min)

# 12 tough questions

**Q1. Did you talk to users?**
No. Desk research only. A 10-person plan is ready.

**Q2. Can you really get notice data?**
Unconfirmed. I'd ask NHAI and IHMCL and fall back to device-side parsing.

**Q3. Why not auto-pay?**
It pays errors and hides the right to dispute.

**Q4. How do you stop fake SMS scams?**
Alerts only in the app, a verified check, and an education screen.

**Q5. What about photo privacy?**
Show only with consent and only a plate crop; never store without a reason.

**Q6. Why 72 hours as the hero?**
It's the rule that costs people money.

**Q7. What did you get wrong?**
I missed the 5-day response rule at first.

**Q8. What if the portal is enough?**
It lets you pay and dispute, but not early alerts, proof or evidence help.

**Q9. Who is the driver in this?**
The person who drives but doesn't own; they see alerts, only the owner pays.

**Q10. What would you cut?**
Fleet bulk pay in v1.

**Q11. How do you measure success?**
E-notices resolved within 72 hours, to be measured.

**Q12. Biggest risk?**
No official data feed.

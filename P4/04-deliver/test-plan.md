# P4 Passline · Validate and plan (plans only; nothing tested yet)

## Six sessions, six tasks
_Planned, not run. 6 participants (5-8): 2 family-car owners, 1 fleet owner, 1 used-car buyer, 1 driver (not owner), 1 person who received an e-notice. 30 min, English/Hindi/Kannada. Prototype: flows A, B on this page._ [In progress]

| Task | Scenario | Success (target) | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Add a car | Add your car and get alerts | Under 2 min, no help: 5 of 6 | Success, time |
| T2 Read the alert | What do you do? How long do you have? | Says '72 hours, pay ₹85': 5 of 6 | Comprehension |
| T3 Check it's real | Is this notice genuine? How do you know? | Finds the Verified badge: 5 of 6 | Success |
| T4 Decide | Is this your car? Pay or dispute. | Chooses with the passage view: 5 of 6 | Errors, think-aloud |
| T5 Dispute | It's a cloned plate. File a dispute. | Files in under 5 min, no help: 4 of 6 | Success, time |
| T6 Check a used car | Check dues before buying | Finds the result: 5 of 6 | Success |

- Measures: SUS (target 80+, to be measured), single ease question, 'Can you spot a fake SMS?' check (target 90%).
- Script: welcome and consent, background, tasks with think-aloud, SUS, closing questions.
- Recording only with written consent. Fictional plates. Right to stop.
- If notes are added in /P4/tests/, I'll show what changed as before/after frames.

# P6 Sooner · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## Six design principles for Sooner
_Used to judge every concept._ [Hypothesis]

**1 Price before dispatch**
A fixed or capped quote, always.

**2 Right kit, plain words**
Basic, Advanced, Neonatal. No jargon.

**3 Three taps**
Panic needs few steps.

**4 Never diagnose**
We ask about needs, never conditions.

**5 Works on weak signal**
SMS and offline fallbacks.

**6 Fair to operators**
Viable prices and quick payment.

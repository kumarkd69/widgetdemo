# P6 Sooner · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## From outcome to candidate solutions
_Outcome at the left, ideas at the right._ [Hypothesis]

- Families get the right ambulance at a fair, known price, fast
  - No obvious first step
    - One-tap request, plain questions
    - Call 108 (free) always shown
  - Price unknown
    - Fixed or capped quote before dispatch
  - Equipment unclear
    - Basic / Advanced / Neonatal in words
  - No ETA or trust in the crew
    - Live ETA with confidence
    - Crew certification card
  - Overcharging after the trip
    - Invoice vs quote check
  - Operators unviable
    - Fair price bands, fast payment, dispatch console

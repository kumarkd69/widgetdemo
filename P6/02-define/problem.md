# P6 Sooner · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## Problem statement and How Might We
_One sentence, then the questions._ [Hypothesis]

Problem: In an emergency, families can't see the price, the equipment level or the arrival time before choosing a private ambulance, and can't tell afterwards whether they were overcharged.

- HMW get a family from panic to a request in three taps?
- HMW show a fixed or capped price before dispatch?
- HMW match equipment to need without asking medical questions?
- HMW show a live ETA, even on weak signal?
- HMW prove the crew is certified?
- HMW compare the final bill with the quote automatically?
- HMW keep the work viable for small operators?

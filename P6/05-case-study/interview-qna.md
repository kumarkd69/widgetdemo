# 12 tough questions

**Q1. Did you talk to users?**
No. Desk research only. A 10-person plan is ready.

**Q2. Are the caps right?**
They're tagged [VERIFY]. Caps differ by state and some are from 2021.

**Q3. Why not bidding?**
Too slow when someone is collapsing.

**Q4. Do you diagnose?**
Never. We ask what the patient needs, not what is wrong.

**Q5. Why a cap, not a fare?**
Distance can change. We tell the family before it does.

**Q6. What if there is no signal?**
ETA and the driver's number go by SMS.

**Q7. Why would operators join?**
Fast payment and a fair price band. Unproven.

**Q8. Who pays?**
Open. Possibly a small fee on completed trips.

**Q9. What did you get wrong?**
I began with an Uber-style map.

**Q10. What would you cut?**
The driver app in v1.

**Q11. How do you measure success?**
Ambulances arriving with the stated kit at the quoted price, to be measured.

**Q12. Biggest risk?**
Showing a wrong or outdated state cap.

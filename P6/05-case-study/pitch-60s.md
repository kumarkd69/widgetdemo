# 60-second pitch

When someone collapses, a family has no time to compare ambulances and no way to know the price or the equipment. I designed Sooner: one red button, three plain questions about what the patient needs, then one card with a price cap, the kit level and the arrival time. After the trip, the bill is checked against the quote. It's a concept built on desk research; next I need operators, interviews and a check of each state's fare caps.

# 10-minute outline

1. Why emergencies break trust (1 min)
2. What the rules say, and where I'm unsure (1 min)
3. Who I designed for (1 min)
4. Three concepts and the choice (2 min)
5. Walkthrough: help, quote, track, bill (3 min)
6. What I can't prove and the test plan (2 min)

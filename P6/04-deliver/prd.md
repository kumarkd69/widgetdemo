# P6 Sooner · Validate and plan (plans only; nothing tested yet)

## Sooner: one page
_Problem, goals, non-goals, requirements, open questions._ [Ready for review]

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Problem | In an emergency, families can't see the price, the equipment level or the arrival time before choosing a private ambulance, and can't tell afterwards whether they were overcharged. |
| Goals | 1) One tap to ask for help, Call 108 always visible. 2) A capped price, kit level and ETA before dispatch. 3) Live tracking with a crew card. 4) Bill checked against the quote. |
| Non-goals | Diagnosing or triaging. Replacing Call 108. Open bidding. Setting legal fare caps. |
| Requirements (MVP) | One-tap request; three need questions; quote card; live ETA with confidence; crew card; SMS fallback; invoice vs quote; operator console; driver app; English, Hindi, Kannada. |
| Success | North Star: share of requests where the ambulance arrives with the stated equipment level at the quoted price (to be measured). |
| Open questions | What are the legal fare caps by state, and does Karnataka bring one under KPME [VERIFY]? How do AIS-125 types map to Basic, Advanced, Neonatal [VERIFY]? Will operators share live fleet data? What licences does an aggregator need [VERIFY]? |

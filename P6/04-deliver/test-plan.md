# P6 Sooner · Validate and plan (plans only; nothing tested yet)

## Six sessions, six tasks
_Planned, not run. 6 participants (5-8): 2 adults who arranged a private ambulance in the last two years, 1 caregiver of an older adult, 1 hospital emergency coordinator, 1 ambulance operator, 1 driver. 30 min, English/Hindi/Kannada. Prototype: flows A and B on this page. Fictional scenarios only; nobody retells a real emergency._ [In progress]

| Task | Scenario | Success (target) | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Ask for help | Your neighbour has collapsed. Get help. | Reaches the quote card in under 3 min: 5 of 6 | Time, success |
| T2 Describe the need | Say what the patient needs | Chooses needs without asking what they mean: 5 of 6 | Success, errors |
| T3 Read the quote | What will you pay, what comes, when? | States cap, kit and ETA: 5 of 6 | Comprehension |
| T4 Weak signal | Signal drops. What do you do? | Finds the SMS path or calls the driver: 5 of 6 | Success |
| T5 Check the bill | The bill is higher. What now? | Finds the flag and the dispute: 5 of 6 | Success |
| T6 Operator view | Who gets request R-101? | Assigns the nearest free vehicle with the right kit: operator participant | Success |

- Measures: SUS (target 80+, to be measured), single ease question, 'I felt in control' rating (1-5, to be measured).
- Script: welcome and consent, background (no medical history), tasks with think-aloud, SUS, closing questions.
- Recording only with written consent. Fictional patients and prices. Right to stop at any time.
- If notes are added in /P6/tests/, I'll show what changed as before/after frames.

# P6 Sooner · Develop

## Three directions, one winner
_Open bidding, fixed quote and a hotline. Scored against the six principles._ [Ready for review]

**A · Live bidding**
- Idea: operators bid on each request and the family picks.
- Storyboard: request → bids arrive → compare → pick.
- Pros: market price discovery.
- Cons: slow in a panic; low bids can mean thin kit; many choices.
- Risk: a race to the bottom hurts operators.

**B · Quote card, then one tap (chosen)**
- Idea: three plain questions, then one card with price cap, equipment level, ETA and crew.
- Storyboard: tap help → answer needs → see the card → confirm → track → bill checked.
- Pros: speed, clarity, fair band for operators.
- Cons: needs operator price bands and live fleet data.
- Risk: bands differ by state [VERIFY].

**C · Concierge hotline**
- Idea: a call centre finds an ambulance and tells you the price.
- Storyboard: call → agent → verbal quote → dispatch.
- Pros: works on any phone.
- Cons: verbal price again; hard to prove afterwards; staffing cost.
- Risk: same trust gap as today.


| Criterion (1-5) | A Bidding | B Quote card | C Hotline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price before dispatch | 3 | 5 | 2 |
| Right kit, plain words | 2 | 5 | 3 |
| Three taps | 2 | 5 | 4 |
| Never diagnose | 3 | 5 | 3 |
| Weak signal | 2 | 4 | 5 |
| Fair to operators | 2 | 4 | 3 |

Decision: build B. A is too slow in an emergency. C keeps today's trust gap, so it stays as an SMS and call fallback inside B.

# P1 Rein · Develop

## Low-fidelity wireframes, MVP flows
_Greyscale. Intent and content priority in each note._ [Ready for review]

- **1 Choose agent**: Intent: know who you're trusting. Priority: badge, then provider.
- **2 Mandate envelope**: Intent: limits first, small defaults. Priority: monthly cap, per payment, where.
- **3 Review mandate**: Intent: show the worst case in rupees. Priority: total at risk.
- **4 Activity feed**: Intent: see it as it happens. Priority: newest, then status.
- **5 Why card**: Intent: a short reason, not a transcript. Priority: request, checks, rule.
- **6 Hold in chat**: Intent: ask only when unusual. Priority: what, where, how much.
- **7 Pause or revoke**: Intent: stop fast. Priority: pause first, revoke second.
- **8 Dispute start**: Intent: name the problem simply. Priority: reason, then evidence note.
- **9 Family view**: Intent: helpers see, owners decide. Priority: who, what they can see.
- **10 Bank dispute console (lo-fi, 1280)**: Intent: all evidence in one view. Priority: mandate, request, checks, timeline.

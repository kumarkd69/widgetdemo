## Four proto-personas
_From the brief and desk research. Replace after real interviews in /P1/interviews/._ [Hypothesis]

**Arjun, 29 · heavy AI and UPI user**
- Goal: let the agent do groceries and bookings
- Context: Android, English, many apps
- Fear: a prompt trick drains his account
- Workaround: approves each payment by hand

**Meena, 58 · cautious, son set up her agent**
- Goal: order medicines without asking her son
- Context: iPhone, Kannada + English, large text
- Fear: not knowing why money moved
- Workaround: calls her son before any payment

**Ravi, 35 · small-business owner**
- Goal: agent pays suppliers on schedule
- Context: laptop + phone, Hindi/English
- Fear: a wrong invoice paid twice
- Workaround: pays manually on Fridays

**Latha, 40 · bank dispute officer**
- Goal: decide disputes fast and fairly
- Context: console, English
- Fear: no evidence of what the agent was told
- Workaround: asks the customer for screenshots

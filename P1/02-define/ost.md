# P1 Rein · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## From outcome to candidate solutions
_Outcome at the left, ideas at the right._ [Hypothesis]

- People let agents pay within limits they trust
  - Setting a safe mandate is hard
    - Mandate envelope with small defaults
    - Templates: groceries, medicines
  - Can't see what agents do
    - Live activity feed
    - Weekly 'was this right?' review
  - No 'why' behind a payment
    - Why card: request, checks, merchant
  - Stopping is slow
    - One-tap pause and revoke
    - Anomaly hold with quick confirm
  - No recourse inside limits
    - Agent dispute flow with evidence pack
  - Trust in the agent
    - Know-Your-Agent badge

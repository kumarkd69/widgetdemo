# P1 Rein · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## Problem statement and How Might We
_One sentence, then the questions._ [Hypothesis]

Problem: People will let AI agents pay within limits they set, but today they cannot see why a payment happened, stop it quickly, or get recourse when it was within limits and still wrong.

- HMW help people set a mandate they understand in under 2 minutes?
- HMW show every agent payment as it happens, with a short reason?
- HMW let anyone pause or revoke in one tap, even for a pending order?
- HMW stop manipulated payments before they settle without nagging on every one?
- HMW give disputes the evidence a bank officer needs?
- HMW let a family member help without taking control?
- HMW show an agent is registered and trustworthy?

# P1 Rein · Validate and plan (plans only; nothing tested yet)

## Six sessions, six tasks
_Planned, not run. 6 participants (5-8): 2 heavy AI users, 1 older user with her family setter (pair), 1 small-business owner, 1 bank dispute officer. 30 min, English/Hindi/Kannada. Prototype: clickable flows A, B and C on this page._ [In progress]

| Task | Scenario | Success (target) | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Set a mandate | Let an agent buy groceries up to ₹5,000 a month | Done in under 2 min, no help: 5 of 6 | Success, time |
| T2 Find the why | Why did the agent pay ₹480 to Zomato? | Finds Why card in under 15 s: 5 of 6 | Time, comprehension |
| T3 Handle a hold | A ₹1,850 payment is held in chat. Decide. | Chooses knowingly; none approve by accident | Errors, think-aloud |
| T4 Pause | Something looks wrong. Stop all agents. | Under 10 seconds: 5 of 6 | Time |
| T5 Dispute | A payment you didn't expect. Start a dispute. | 3 steps, no help: 5 of 6 | Success |
| T6 Officer review | Read the evidence and decide D-2207 | Decides in under 2 min: officer + 1 proxy | Time, confidence |

- Measures: SUS (target 80+, to be measured), single ease question per task, 'What is the most you could lose this month?' check (target 90% correct).
- Script: welcome and consent, background, tasks with think-aloud, SUS, closing questions.
- Recording only with written consent. No real accounts or amounts.
- If notes are added in /P1/tests/, I'll show what changed as before/after frames.

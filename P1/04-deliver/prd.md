# P1 Rein · Validate and plan (plans only; nothing tested yet)

## Rein: one page
_Problem, goals, non-goals, requirements, open questions._ [Ready for review]

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Problem | People will let AI agents pay within limits they set, but can't see why, stop quickly, or get recourse when it's within limits and wrong. |
| Goals | 1) Mandate set in under 2 minutes. 2) Every agent payment visible with a short why. 3) Pause in under 10 seconds. 4) A dispute path with evidence. |
| Non-goals | Rein never authorises or settles payments (rules do). No model reasoning transcript. No auto-raising limits. Not an agent. |
| Requirements (MVP) | Agent list with registry badge; mandate envelope; live feed; Why card; one-tap pause and revoke; anomaly hold in chat; dispute with evidence pack; bank console view; English + Hindi + Kannada. |
| Success | North Star: share of agent payments the owner marks 'expected' within 7 days (to be measured). |
| Open questions | Who is liable inside the mandate? Do agents get separate limits under UAP? What dispute reason code applies? Is there a registry API? When does RBI approve UAP? |

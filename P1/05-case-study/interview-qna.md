# 12 tough questions

**Q1. Did you talk to users?**
No. Desk research only. A 10-person plan is ready and I label everything hypothesis.

**Q2. Isn't UPI Circle enough?**
It gives limits and revocation. It has no agent identity, no why and no dispute path for 'within limits but wrong'.

**Q3. Who is liable?**
Unresolved publicly. I design the evidence pack so any answer can be applied.

**Q4. Why not show the model's reasoning?**
Too long to read, and NPCI says authorisation must be deterministic. The checks are what matter.

**Q5. What stops prompt manipulation?**
A merchant allowlist and a hold for new merchants. I don't claim it is solved.

**Q6. Will people approve holds blindly?**
That's the risk. Holds are rare, buttons are equal, and E2 tests it.

**Q7. Why small default limits?**
Limits before trust. Raising them is deliberate.

**Q8. How does a bank use this?**
A console view with mandate, request, checks and timeline.

**Q9. What did you get wrong?**
I began with permissions. The problem is recourse.

**Q10. What would you cut?**
Family view and templates.

**Q11. How do you measure success?**
Share of agent payments marked expected, to be measured.

**Q12. Biggest risk?**
UAP timing and liability.

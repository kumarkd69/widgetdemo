# 60-second pitch

AI agents will soon pay for people on UPI, inside limits. Today you can't see why a payment happened, stop it fast, or dispute it when it was within limits but wrong. I designed Rein: a small mandate with the worst case in rupees, a live feed with a three-line why, holds only when something is unusual, one-tap pause, and a dispute with evidence your bank can read. It's a concept with desk research and a prototype; next I need interviews and a bank partner.

# 10-minute outline

1. Why agent payments change risk (1 min)
2. What NPCI has said (1 min)
3. Who I designed for (1 min)
4. Three concepts and the choice (2 min)
5. Walkthrough: mandate, feed, why, hold, pause (3 min)
6. What I can't prove and the test plan (2 min)

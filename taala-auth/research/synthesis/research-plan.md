## Discussion guide (45 min)
1. **Warm-up (5):** Which apps do you use to pay? Show me your home screen.
2. **Last payment (10):** Walk me through the last time you paid. What did you have to enter? Anything slow or confusing?
3. **When it went wrong (10):** Tell me about a time a payment failed or you got locked out. What did you do? Who helped?
4. **Fraud (10):** Have you or someone you know received a fraud call or SMS? How did you know? What makes a payment screen look "real" to you?
5. **Words (5):** Card sort: Verify / Confirm / Approve / Authorise / Authenticate — which means what to you? In Kannada/Hindi?
6. **Close (5):** If your bank could change one thing about how you prove it's you, what would it be?

Never ask for PINs, OTPs, account numbers or screenshots with balances.

## Moderated usability test (later, on Figma prototype)
- **Tasks:** (1) Pay ₹450 at a shop; fingerprint fails. (2) Send ₹2,00,000 to a new payee at night. (3) Approve an ₹18,000 foreign purchase with Indian SIM off. (4) You're locked out — get back in. (5) Set up a passkey after a payment.
- **Measures:** task success, time on task, errors, number of retries before fallback, SEQ (1–7), trust rating (1–7), comprehension of the step-up reason (teach-back).
- **Conditions:** H1 explainer on/off between-subjects; languages per participant's choice.

## Consent and ethics
- Written or recorded verbal consent in the participant's language; purpose, recording, storage, right to stop.
- No real credentials, no real money. Prototype uses fake data.
- Store recordings encrypted, delete after 90 days; anonymise quotes (P1–P12).
- Pay an honorarium; for older and low-vision participants, allow a family member to be present but not to answer for them.
- If a participant reveals they're being defrauded, stop and point them to 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in.

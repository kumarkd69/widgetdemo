# P7 Span · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## Six design principles for Span
_Used to judge every concept._ [Hypothesis]

**1 Dates first**
Your cover ends on a date. Show it.

**2 Show what you keep**
Credit as a number, per person.

**3 Calm urgency**
Clear deadlines, no fear.

**4 Neutral on insurers**
Show routes, not a favourite.

**5 Health data last, least**
Ask for it only when needed.

**6 The whole family**
Everyone covered is shown.

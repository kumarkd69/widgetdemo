# P7 Span · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## Problem statement and How Might We
_One sentence, then the questions._ [Hypothesis]

Problem: People who rely on employer health cover discover it ends only after they leave, and the short window to keep waiting-period credit is buried in HR emails and policy documents.

- HMW tell people the exact date their cover ends, early and in plain words?
- HMW show what they lose and what they can keep, in numbers?
- HMW compare migration, portability and a fresh policy without bias?
- HMW collect the least health data needed to apply?
- HMW cover the whole family, not just the employee?
- HMW help HR send people the right option at exit?
- HMW use urgency without scaring people?

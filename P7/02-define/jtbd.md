# P7 Span · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## Eight job stories
_When... I want to... so I can..._ [Hypothesis]

- When I resign or retire, I want to see the exact date my cover ends, so I can plan.
- When my cover is about to end, I want to see what I lose and what I keep, so I'm not surprised.
- When I compare options, I want waiting-period credit shown as a number, so I can see what moving costs.
- When I decide, I want a short checklist of documents, so I don't miss a step.
- When I apply, I want a pre-filled form that asks for the least health data, so it's quick and private.
- When I'm responsible for my family, I want to see each member's cover and credit, so nobody falls through.
- When the deadline nears, I want calm reminders, so I act without panic.
- When I'm in HR, I want exit workflows that tell people their options, so no one is left uninsured.

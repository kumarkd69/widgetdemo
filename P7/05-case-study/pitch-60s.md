# 60-second pitch

Many Indians have health cover only through an employer, and it ends when the job does. People find out after. I designed Span: your cover-cliff timeline with real dates, what you keep as a number per person, three routes compared honestly, and an application that asks for the least health data. It's a concept built on desk research; next I need interviews and an insurer to verify the windows.

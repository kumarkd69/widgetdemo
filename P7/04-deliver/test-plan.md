# P7 Span · Validate and plan (plans only; nothing tested yet)

## Six sessions, six tasks
_Planned, not run. 6 participants (5-8): 2 about to leave a job (one laid off), 1 within a year of retirement with a chronic condition, 1 starting a business, 1 spouse or dependant, 1 HR benefits manager. 30 min, English/Hindi/Kannada. Prototype: flows A, B on this page. Avoid asking for diagnoses._ [In progress]

| Task | Scenario | Success (target) | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Read your date | When does your cover end? When must you act? | States both dates: 5 of 6 | Comprehension |
| T2 What you keep | What do you keep if you move to an individual policy? | Names credit and family: 5 of 6 | Comprehension |
| T3 Choose a route | Pick a route and say why | Gives a reason using credit or cost: 5 of 6 | Think-aloud |
| T4 Prepare | What's missing before you can apply? | Finds the policy copy: 5 of 6 | Success |
| T5 Apply | Complete the application | Done in under 5 min; comfortable with health questions: 4 of 6 | Success, comfort rating |
| T6 HR view | Who needs a reminder today? | Finds the right person: HR participant | Success |

- Measures: SUS (target 80+, to be measured), single ease question, 'I felt calm' rating (1-5, to be measured).
- Script: welcome and consent, background (no diagnoses), tasks with think-aloud, SUS, closing questions.
- Recording only with written consent. Fictional policies and dates. Right to stop.

# P7 Span · Validate and plan (plans only; nothing tested yet)

## Span: one page
_Problem, goals, non-goals, requirements, open questions._ [Ready for review]

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Problem | People who rely on employer health cover find out it ends only after they leave, and the short window to keep waiting-period credit is buried in HR emails and policy documents. |
| Goals | 1) Show the exact date cover ends. 2) Show what you keep, in numbers, per person. 3) Compare migrate, port, fresh. 4) Apply with the least health data. |
| Non-goals | Auto-migrating people. Scoring health risk. Recommending one insurer. Replacing HR or insurer systems. |
| Requirements (MVP) | Exit date entry or HR invite; cliff timeline; keep/lose/change; credit per member; three routes with 'confirm with insurer' flags; document checklist; reminders; English + Hindi + Kannada. |
| Success | North Star: share of leavers with continuous cover 30 days after exit (to be measured). |
| Open questions | What are the real windows by insurer and route? Can portability go to another insurer from a group policy? Will HR share exit dates? Which insurers support pre-filled applications? |

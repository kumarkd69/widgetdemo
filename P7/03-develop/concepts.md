# P7 Span · Develop

## Three directions, one winner
_Automated, guided and human-assisted. Scored against the six principles._ [Ready for review]

**A · Auto-migrate at exit**
- Idea: HR passes the exit date; the employee is moved to an individual plan with the same insurer by default.
- Storyboard: exit date logged → plan created → premium bill.
- Pros: no gap.
- Cons: spends the employee's money without a choice; ignores better options.
- Risk: consent and mis-selling.

**B · Cover-cliff timeline with a guided choice (chosen)**
- Idea: your dates, what you keep and lose, credit per person, three routes compared, checklist and pre-filled application.
- Storyboard: open invite → see the date → see what you keep → choose → apply.
- Pros: clarity, control, family view.
- Cons: depends on HR data and insurer terms.
- Risk: windows differ by insurer [VERIFY].

**C · Human case manager**
- Idea: a broker or case manager calls and does it for you.
- Storyboard: book → call → forms signed.
- Pros: warmth.
- Cons: cost, conflict of interest, availability at exit.
- Risk: pushes one insurer.


| Criterion (1-5) | A Auto | B Timeline | C Manager |
|---|---|---|---|

| Show what you keep | 2 | 5 | 3 |

Decision: build B. A is rejected: choosing a policy for someone removes consent and hides options. C becomes an optional advisor call later. Logged in DECISIONS.md.

## Span: keeping health cover when you leave a job or retire
_People find out they're uninsured after they've left the job, often during a health event. The window to keep waiting-period credit is short and buried in HR emails and policy documents nobody reads. Desk research only._ [Ready for review]

## What the rules say about group exit and portability
_Cited facts and what each means for design._ [Hypothesis]

| Fact | Source | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| IRDAI Master Circular on Health Insurance Business (29 May 2024): every member of a group indemnity policy must be offered migration to an individual or family floater policy on exit, on modification or withdrawal of the group policy. | IRDAI, TaxGuru | Surface the migration option on the exit date, not just in a policy PDF |
| On migration with the same insurer, credits carry over to the extent of the sum insured: no-claim bonus, specific waiting periods, pre-existing-disease waiting and moratorium. Only the unexpired residual waiting period applies. | IRDAI circular, Ditto | Show 'waiting-period credit' as a number, per family member |
| Portability from group to an individual policy of another insurer: sources conflict (some say any insurer, others say only the same insurer). | Ditto, HDFC Ergo, Tata AIG | Mark as [VERIFY]; show both routes with their conditions |
| Standard portability between individual policies: apply at least 45 days and not more than 60 days before renewal; the insurer must share history within 7 working days; the new insurer must respond within 15 days or it counts as accepted [VERIFY]. | Ditto, Policybazaar, Bajaj General | Reminder cadence must start well before the exit date |
| PED waiting capped at 36 months (down from 48); moratorium 5 years (down from 8); credits and moratorium continue on portability [VERIFY effective dates]. | Business Today, Nyvo | 'You've served X of 5 years' is a design object |
| Group cover typically ends when employment ends. Insurers say to apply 30-45 days before expiry or within 30 days of leaving [VERIFY]; timing is not uniform. | Outlook Money, Tata AIG | Show the earliest and latest dates with 'confirm with insurer' |
| Older employees and retirees face higher premiums, underwriting and PED waits if they move late. | Insurer pages, trade coverage | Urgency without fear: quantify what moving late costs |

Correction to the brief: the brief says migration is allowed 'if applied for within a window before the cover ends'. The circular says the option must be offered at exit; the application window varies by insurer and by route. What the rules don't say: a single deadline that applies to everyone; who tells the employee, and when; whether HR must share the exit date with the insurer.

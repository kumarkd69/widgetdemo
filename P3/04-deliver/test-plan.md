# P3 Mend · Validate and plan (plans only; nothing tested yet)

## Six sessions, six tasks
_Planned, not run. 6 participants (5-8): 2 past victims (6+ months ago, willing), 1 family helper, 1 older adult, 1 bank fraud staff, 1 low-literacy user. 30 min. Fictional scenario only, no real losses. A support contact is offered in every session._ [In progress]

| Task | Scenario | Success (target) | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Start | You've just been scammed. What do you do first? | Taps the big button in under 10 s: 5 of 6 | Time |
| T2 Call and save | Call 1930 (simulated) and keep the number | Saves the number without help: 5 of 6 | Success |
| T3 Tell the bank | Tell your bank what happened | Uses the ready message: 5 of 6 | Success |
| T4 Evidence | Add evidence and answer a permission prompt | Understands why permission is asked: 5 of 6 | Comprehension |
| T5 What's next | Look at the case. What's your next step? | Names the next step: 5 of 6 | Comprehension |
| T6 Expectations | Read 'What to expect'. Will you get money back? | Says 'not guaranteed': 6 of 6 | Paraphrase |

- Measures: SUS (target 80+, to be measured), single ease question, a 1-5 'I felt supported' rating (to be measured).
- Script: welcome and consent, scenario read-out, tasks with think-aloud, SUS, closing questions, support contact.
- Recording only with written consent. Fictional amounts. Right to stop at any time.
- If notes are added in /P3/tests/, I'll show what changed as before/after frames.

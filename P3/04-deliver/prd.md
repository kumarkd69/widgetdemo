# P3 Mend · Validate and plan (plans only; nothing tested yet)

## Mend: one page
_Problem, goals, non-goals, requirements, open questions._ [Ready for review]

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Problem | In the first hour after fraud, victims must work four unconnected systems with no single case, organised evidence, status or honest expectations. |
| Goals | 1) First action in under 10 seconds. 2) Every case number in one place. 3) Evidence collected by default. 4) Honest status and expectations. |
| Non-goals | Promising refunds. Filing on the victim's behalf. Giving legal advice. Scoring recovery odds. Replacing 1930, NCRP or the bank. |
| Requirements (MVP) | 'I was scammed' start; triage; call 1930 with script; case number vault; tell-bank and file-portal helpers; case river (manual); evidence checklist; honest expectations; English + Hindi + Kannada. |
| Success | North Star: share of victims who finish the first-hour checklist within 60 minutes of opening Mend (to be measured). |
| Open questions | Can agencies send status to Mend? Is the Money Restoration Module open to apps? What is the correct wording on liability? Who vets the helpline numbers shown? |

# P3 Mend · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## North Star, HEART, guardrails
_Baselines are 'to be measured'. Nothing here is a result._ [Hypothesis]

North Star: share of victims who finish the first-hour checklist (1930 called, bank informed, NCRP filed) within 60 minutes of opening Mend. Target to set after baseline.

| HEART | Metric | Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| Happiness | 'I knew what to do next' rating (1-5) | To be measured |
| Engagement | Cases with evidence added | To be measured |
| Adoption | Opens leading to a 1930 call | To be measured |
| Retention | Cases opened that are still updated at day 30 | To be measured |
| Task success | Case numbers saved for all channels | To be measured |

| Guardrail | Why |
|---|---|
| Refund promises made = 0 | We can't promise recovery |
| Evidence privacy incidents = 0 | Sensitive data |
| Time to first action under 10 seconds | Speed in crisis |
| Users who say the app made them feel blamed = 0 | Tone |

# P3 Mend · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## Problem statement and How Might We
_One sentence, then the questions._ [Hypothesis]

Problem: In the first hour after being defrauded, victims must work four unconnected systems with no single case, no organised evidence, no status afterwards and no honest idea of what can be recovered.

- HMW get a shaken person to the right first action in under 10 seconds?
- HMW keep every case number and acknowledgement in one place?
- HMW collect evidence without asking a stressed person to hunt for it?
- HMW show where each channel stands in plain words?
- HMW be honest about recovery without crushing hope?
- HMW let a family member help while the victim stays in charge?
- HMW teach prevention without blaming?

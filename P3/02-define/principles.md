# P3 Mend · Define (hypotheses from desk research; no interviews yet)

## Six design principles for Mend
_Used to judge every concept._ [Hypothesis]

**1 One thing at a time**
One big action per screen in the first hour.

**2 Honest hope**
Never promise a refund; say what happens next.

**3 Speed first**
Report first, complete later.

**4 Evidence by default**
Collect it without being asked.

**5 Steady voice**
Short, kind sentences. No blame.

**6 Shared, not taken over**
Helpers help; the victim decides.

# 60-second pitch

After a UPI scam you have about an hour that matters and four systems to work. I designed Mend: one big button, a script for 1930, a saved case number, a ready message for your bank, and one case river that shows every step. It never promises a refund. It's a concept built on desk research; next I need careful interviews with past victims and a bank partner.

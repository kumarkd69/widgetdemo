# 12 tough questions

**Q1. Did you talk to victims?**
No. Desk research only. I wrote a careful plan with support contacts.

**Q2. Can you get money back for users?**
No, and the app says so. Recovery depends on banks and police.

**Q3. Why not show a refund countdown?**
It may not apply to authorised payments, and it adds pressure.

**Q4. Is Mend an official channel?**
No. It points to 1930, the portal and the bank and keeps the case together.

**Q5. How does the case river get status?**
Manually today. If agencies offer data, I'd add it.

**Q6. What about privacy of evidence?**
Stored on device, shared only by choice. Guardrail: zero incidents.

**Q7. Why not file the complaint for the victim?**
A wrong complaint in their name is risky. Mend pre-fills; they file.

**Q8. How do you handle distress?**
Short sentences, no blame, a support option, no scary timers.

**Q9. What if scammers copy Mend?**
One official link, no phone numbers except 1930, warnings.

**Q10. What did you get wrong?**
The refund clock.

**Q11. What would you cut?**
Prevention lessons and mule-freeze flow for v1.

**Q12. Biggest risk?**
Wrong or outdated links and numbers; I'd verify every release.

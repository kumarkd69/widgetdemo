# P3 Mend · Develop

## Three directions, one winner
_Automated, guided and human-assisted. Scored against the six principles._ [Ready for review]

**A · Auto-pilot**
- Idea: one tap, and the app calls 1930, sends facts and letters from data on the phone.
- Storyboard: tap → app acts → victim reads a summary.
- Pros: fastest.
- Cons: acts for a shaken person without a clear choice; needs deep access we may not get.
- Risk: a wrong or half-true complaint filed in the victim's name.

**B · Guided first hour + case river (chosen)**
- Idea: one big action at a time, a saved case with every number, an evidence vault and a status river.
- Storyboard: tap 'I was scammed' → call 1930 → save number → tell bank → file → case river.
- Pros: fast, kind, victim stays in charge; works without agency integrations.
- Cons: status is manual until agencies integrate.
- Risk: victims stop after the first call.

**C · Human-assisted**
- Idea: a trained volunteer calls back and walks through every step.
- Storyboard: tap 'Call me' → volunteer guides → notes saved.
- Pros: warmth, trust.
- Cons: expensive, hard to scale, privacy exposure.
- Risk: volunteers give legal advice.


| Criterion (1-5) | A Auto-pilot | B Guided | C Assisted |
|---|---|---|---|
| One thing at a time | 3 | 5 | 4 |
| Honest hope | 2 | 5 | 4 |
| Speed first | 5 | 4 | 2 |
| Evidence by default | 3 | 5 | 3 |
| Steady voice | 2 | 4 | 5 |
| Shared, not taken over | 2 | 4 | 4 |
| Fits North Star (first-hour checklist) | 4 | 5 | 3 |
| Total (out of 35) | 21 | 32 | 25 |

Decision: build B. Add a human call-back later through a partner. A is rejected: filing for a shaken person without a clear choice is risky. Logged in DECISIONS.md.

# P4 · MLFF toll e-notice assistant
Working name: "TollClear" (rename if it collides)

## Context (verify)
- Multi-Lane Free Flow tolling uses ANPR cameras + FASTag; vehicles pass without stopping.
- National Highways Fee (Second Amendment) Rules, 2026: unpaid passages trigger an e-notice to the registered owner (vehicle, date, place, amount). Pay the normal toll within 72 hours; after that it's twice the toll.
- Representation (dispute) can be filed on the designated portal within 72 hours of the e-notice; after 15 days unpaid with no dispute, dues are recorded on VAHAN and block services such as fitness, transfer or re-registration.
- e-notice portal: nhfeenotice.parivahan.gov.in. Central Motor Vehicles Rules 2026 amendment: NOC only after clearing pending dues.
Sources: newsonair.gov.in (19 Mar 2026), NHAI on X (2 May 2026), tollguru.com India guide.

## Problem
Tolling became invisible. When a payment silently fails or a camera misreads a plate, the owner finds out late, pays double, or discovers a block on their vehicle months later when selling it.

## Users
Suresh (45, family car, son drives it), Lakshmi (38, fleet owner with 12 cabs), Farhan (30, bought a used car with hidden dues), internal: NHAI grievance officer.

## Scope ideas
Proactive alert within minutes, "is this really my car?" evidence view (photo, gantry, time), one-tap pay within 72 h, guided dispute with evidence (cloned plate, wrong class, FASTag debited twice), fleet dashboard, pre-purchase dues check for used cars, trip-level toll preview.

## Trade-offs
Alert fatigue vs missed notices; showing ANPR photos (privacy) vs proof; default auto-pay vs dispute rights.

## Surfaces
Mobile 390, fleet web 1440, in-car glance (optional).
## Theme
Road-sign clarity: asphalt grey + highway green + signal yellow; signature visual = a route ribbon with gantries and the 72-hour clock.

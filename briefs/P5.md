# P5 · Buying insurance on Bima Sugam without an agent
Working name: "Samjho" ("understand"; rename if it collides)

## Context (verify)
- Bima Sugam is IRDAI's unified insurance marketplace run by the Bima Sugam India Federation (Section 8 not-for-profit).
- Phased rollout in 2026: motor first (reported July), health and motor renewals (August), term life (September); exact live status must be checked on bimasugam.co.in.
- Zero-commission model: insurers pay a platform fee (reported 5–7%) instead of agent commissions; Bima Pehchaan gives one-time KYC and a single dashboard of all policies.
- Private aggregators continue to operate; distribution economics are shifting.
Sources: bimasugam.co.in, IRDAI press releases, Business Standard, Vaatun briefing (May 2026).

## Problem
Agents and brokers, for all their flaws, explained policies and helped at claim time. A zero-commission marketplace removes that person. People now compare dozens of policies with jargon (sub-limits, co-pay, waiting periods, room-rent caps, IDV) and no one to ask.

## Users
Kavya (27, first job, buying her first health policy), Ramesh (52, renewing family floater, worried about pre-existing conditions), Lakshmi (38, renewing 12 commercial vehicle policies), internal: insurer product manager, IRDAI grievance cell (stakeholder).

## Scope ideas
Needs-first questionnaire (not product-first), plain-language "what this policy does NOT cover", side-by-side comparison with honest trade-offs, scenario simulator ("what if I'm hospitalised for 5 days for X"), neutral ranking rules shown openly, Bima Pehchaan portfolio with gap detection, renewal coaching, "talk to a licensed advisor" fallback.

## Trade-offs
Neutrality vs recommending; simplicity vs full disclosure; personalisation vs data minimisation (DPDP); sponsored placement must never exist.

## Surfaces
Mobile 390, web 1440.
## Theme
Clear and trustworthy: deep green + cream; signature visual = a "coverage shape" that shows gaps at a glance.

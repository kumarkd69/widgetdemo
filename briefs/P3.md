# P3 · Fraud victim recovery companion
Working name: "Wapas" (rename if it collides)

## Context (verify)
- Victims are told to call 1930 within the first hour, report on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), inform their bank, and go to police; these are separate channels.
- RBI zero-liability: reporting unauthorised transactions within 3 working days limits customer liability (verify current direction and conditions).
- The Money Restoration Module became functional in April 2026 under the NCRP-CFCFRMS framework; a 2026 SOP covers restoration routes; a hold stops money moving but restoration is a separate process with no universal timeline.
- Govt reported the system helped prevent over ₹11,158 crore being siphoned across 32.80 lakh complaints (as of 30 Jun 2026; verify).
- Experts note reporting is fast now; investigation and recovery lag.
Sources: cybercrime.gov.in, MHA/I4C press releases, RBI customer liability circular, Business Standard (25 Jun 2026).

## Problem
In the worst hour of their financial life, victims must work four separate systems, gather evidence they didn't know to keep, and then wait months with no visibility.

## Users
Meena (58, lost ₹2.4 lakh to a "digital arrest" call), Arjun (29, UPI collect-request scam, ₹18,000), Imran (41, shop owner whose account got frozen as a mule — secondary scenario), internal: bank fraud desk, cyber cell officer (as stakeholders, not users of our app).

## Scope ideas
Panic-mode first 60 minutes (one big action at a time), evidence vault (auto-collect UTR, screenshots, call log), one case across channels with acknowledgement numbers, status timeline, letters/emails pre-drafted, zero-liability deadline clock, emotional support and family helper mode, scam-pattern education after the crisis.

## Trade-offs
Speed vs completeness; calm tone vs urgency; we can't promise recovery; data privacy of evidence; integrations we don't control.

## Surfaces
Mobile 390 (primary), helper web 1440.
## Theme
Steady and kind: navy + soft coral; signature visual = a "golden hour" countdown and a case river showing each agency's status.

# P2 · Consumer consent manager under the DPDP Act
Working name: "Haq" (rename if it collides)

## Context (verify)
- DPDP Rules, 2025 notified 13 Nov 2025; Rule 4 (Consent Manager registration) operative 13 Nov 2026; Rule 3 notice obligations and most duties by 13 May 2027.
- A Consent Manager is a registered, data-blind intermediary that lets people give, review and withdraw consent through one interoperable platform; using one is optional for companies. Minimum net worth ₹2 crore.
- Reports in Aug 2026 said the Data Protection Board was not fully constituted and registration wasn't operating yet.
- Related: Account Aggregator / DEPA consent patterns already exist in finance.
Sources: DPDP Rules 2025 gazette (MeitY), the DPDP Act 2023 text, Sahamati/DEPA docs for prior art.

## Problem
People have shared data with hundreds of apps and can't see, understand or withdraw any of it. The law creates a new kind of product to fix this, and nobody has designed it for real Indian users yet.

## Users
Priya (34, privacy-aware professional); Imran (41, shop owner, low literacy, many apps installed by others); Anitha (45, parent of a 14-year-old; children's data needs verifiable parental consent — verify the rule); internal: a company's privacy officer (data fiduciary side).

## Scope ideas
Consent inventory, plain-language purpose cards, withdraw with real confirmation (processing stopped, not a flag), rights requests (access, correction, erasure, grievance, nominee), notifications when consent is used, child/guardian view, fiduciary integration console.

## Trade-offs
Granularity vs overload; data-blind design vs helpful insights; neutrality vs nudging; how to show proof that a company complied.

# P7 · Keeping health cover when you leave a job or retire
Working name: "Bridge" (rename if it collides)

## Context (verify all)
- Many Indians are covered only by employer group health insurance, which ends when employment ends.
- IRDAI rules allow migration from a group policy to an individual policy with the same insurer and portability to another insurer, with credit for waiting periods already served, if applied for within a window before the cover ends (verify current master circular wording, timelines and conditions).
- Older employees and retirees face pre-existing condition waiting periods, higher premiums, and medical underwriting if they move late.
Sources: IRDAI Master Circular on Health Insurance (29 May 2024), insurer migration/portability pages, IRDAI consumer education site.

## Problem
People discover they're uninsured after they've left the job, often during a health event. The window to keep waiting-period credit is short and buried in HR emails and policy documents nobody reads.

## Users
Suresh (58, retiring in 3 months, diabetic, wife covered too), Kavya (27, laid off, between jobs), Imran (41, leaving a job to start a business), HR benefits manager (stakeholder and potential distribution partner).

## Scope ideas
Coverage cliff timeline ("your cover ends on 30 Nov; act by 15 Nov"), what you lose vs keep, migration vs portability vs fresh policy comparison with waiting-period credit shown, family members' coverage, document checklist and pre-filled application, HR integration for exit workflows, reminder cadence.

## Trade-offs
Urgency vs fear; recommending an insurer vs staying neutral; HR's interests vs the employee's; handling sensitive health data minimally.

# P1 · AI agent payment controls on UPI
Working name: "Leash" (rename if it collides with a brand)

## Context (verify)
- NPCI is building the Unified Agent Protocol (UAP) to register, verify and authorise AI agents to make UPI payments; it builds on UPI Circle delegation and needs RBI approval before launch.
- It is expected to let agents make small payments within user-set limits, without approving each one. Existing UPI Circle full-delegation limits are reported as ₹5,000 per transaction and ₹15,000 per month; Reserve Pay can block up to ₹10,000 for 90 days (verify; limits may change for agents).
- Agentic UPI pilots: ChatGPT (with Razorpay, from Oct 2025) and Claude (Feb 2026, with Zomato, Swiggy, Zepto as early partners).
- NPCI's chairman said at GFF (10 Sep 2026) that AI may decide intent but authorisation and settlement must stay deterministic. Liability when an agent pays within its mandate but against the user's interest is unresolved.
Sources to read: inc42.com (NPCI agentic payments), Business Standard (UAP, 9 Jul 2026), NPCI press releases on UPI Circle and Reserve Pay.

## Problem
People are about to give AI agents permission to spend their money. Today's controls were built for family delegation, not software that acts on its own, makes mistakes, or gets manipulated by a merchant's prompt.

## Users
Arjun (29, heavy AI and UPI user, wants convenience); Meena (58, cautious, her son set up an agent for her); Ravi (35, small-business owner letting an agent pay suppliers); internal: risk analyst, bank dispute officer.

## Scope ideas
Mandate creation (who, what, how much, how often, which merchants, expiry), live agent activity feed, "why did my agent pay this?" explanations, anomaly holds, one-tap pause/revoke, dispute flow for "within limits but wrong", a Know-Your-Agent trust badge, and family view.

## Key trade-offs to explore
Autonomy vs control; per-transaction approval fatigue vs risk; how much reasoning to show; liability wording; defaults for first-time users.

## Surfaces
Mobile app 390, agent-chat interstitials, bank dispute console 1440.
## Theme
Calm and precise: deep teal + signal amber; signature visual = a "mandate envelope" showing spent vs allowed.
